Overview

The Rating Dial is a single-axis input device for continuous ratings during fMRI: pain, craving, affect, arousal, intensity, or any visual analog scale (VAS) task. The participant turns a knob with two fingers, like a volume control, and the rotation appears to your stimulus software as a smooth 0–1023 axis value. Two buttons, one on each side of the dial, can be used for confirmations, onset markers or event responses.

It is built on a heavy base so it can be rested on a thigh or tray without being held down, and the knob is operated with small finger movements that keep the forearm and head still. It presents itself to the computer as a standard USB game controller (a mouse mode is also available), so it works without drivers.

Features

  • One rotary axis, 0–1023 over the full travel, with hard end stops
  • Two buttons, debounced in firmware
  • Appears as a USB joystick (X axis plus buttons 1 and 2); no drivers needed
  • Firmware filtering to suppress electrical noise and keep the signal steady at rest
  • Free browser-based configurator (Chrome or Edge) for calibration, filtering and inversion; no software to install
  • Free demo continuous rating task you can run in a browser to check your setup
  • Non-ferrous construction in the parts near the participant; heavy plastic base
  • Two-finger knob; knob diameter and grip are adjustable on request
  • Works with software that reads a joystick or game controller, such as E-Prime
  • For research use only

Please have your MR safety officer review the device and test it in your own environment before use with participants. We can supply material information on request.
